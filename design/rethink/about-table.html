What drives me
i.
AI that meets felt needs

Demos answer whether you can build the concept. Production answers whether the app meets the need. I spend my time on both.

ii.
Writing as external memory

Sixty-five articles because future-me will forget. That others find them useful is a side effect I’m glad about.

iii.
Open source as paying rent

LlamaIndex, Unsloth, IBM ACP, Chainlit, Giskard. Use a framework long enough and you find the bug. File the issue. Send the PR.

iv.
Making AI legible

Most AI projects die in the gap between what the system can do and what the organisation believes it can do. I spend as much time on that gap as on the code.

What it is

A full production-realistic stack, built to exercise a real multi-service architecture end to end.

FastAPI, LangGraph, Celery, RabbitMQ, Redis Sentinel, Citus distributed Postgres, dual-pool PgBouncer, PostgREST, ClickHouse, Langfuse, MinIO, an LGTM observability stack, and a Next.js frontend behind Nginx. Solo-built and vibe-coded end to end with LLMs, then audited by CodeRabbit.

Why it looks like this
Constraint

Solo, no team. It had to prove real distributed-systems work, not a toy demo.

Decision

Build the full stack anyway: queues, caches, distributed Postgres, observability and a proxy.

Outcome

90%+ test coverage, CodeRabbit-audited, public repo.

At work · Illumina

Illumina Agentic RAG

A sixteen-component production retrieval platform

Private, built at work
Highlight
$9M anticipated savings
16
components between a question and its answer
What it is

Production agentic RAG for enterprise field-service engineering.

Semantic double-merge chunking, chunk dreaming, HyDE, hybrid search, Cohere reranking, prompt compression, and an iterative multi-hop retrieval loop. A blue-green swap of vector collections, worked out from first principles, keeps reindexing safe.

Why it looks like this
Constraint

Sole data scientist, production SLAs, no ML team to lean on for retrieval quality.

Decision

Sixteen stages, each earning its place, plus a blue-green collection swap for safe reindexing.

Outcome

Anticipated to save $9M upon full deployment.
